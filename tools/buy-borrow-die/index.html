Buy, borrow, die.
Run your numbers before you borrow.

The wealthy rarely sell stock to live on. They borrow against it, the loan isn't income, and if they hold until death the gains are never taxed. This calculator tests whether that works at your size, and where it breaks.

Buy the calculator · $29
What it does
  • Borrow or sell, side by side. The same yearly spending, funded two ways: draws on a securities-backed line, or selling shares and paying the tax. Hundreds of market paths run both ways at once.
  • Every loan-to-value cap from 0% to 60%. For each one: expected ending net worth, the chance of at least one margin call, and the shortfall against selling in a bad market.
  • Margin calls as they really work. When the loan crosses the lender's limit, shares are sold to get back under it, and the tax on those gains comes out of the sale.
  • Your rate, your terms. Rates tiered by portfolio size or a fixed rate, interest paid or added to the loan, your call and approval limits, your cost basis.
  • The step-up at death. One switch shows what the estate keeps when the gains are wiped out, and what it still owes the lender.
  • Export to CSV for your own spreadsheet or your adviser.
What you get

One file. It opens in any web browser, works offline, and runs entirely on your computer: nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. A one-time purchase with nothing billed later. After you pay, you go straight to the download page.

Who it's for

Anyone with a taxable brokerage account who has heard about borrowing instead of selling and wants to see the trade-off in numbers, not a sales pitch. It doesn't cover IRAs or 401(k)s, because retirement accounts can't be pledged as collateral.
