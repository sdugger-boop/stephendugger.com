Tool · Reserve Builder

A reserve is what keeps
one bad month from becoming debt.

Two funds, in order. First a sinking fund for the big expenses you can see coming: registration, insurance premiums, tires, the holidays. Then an emergency fund for the ones you can't. The sinking fund comes first because otherwise every planned expense raids the emergency fund, and it never fills.
