The 120-day rule comes first. To keep CalPERS health in retirement, your pension has to start within 120 days of your last day on the job, from an agency that covers your group. Miss it and the coverage can't be reinstated. A safety member can't retire before 50, so leaving before 50 means no CalPERS retiree health unless you go to another CalPERS employer and retire from there. This tool flags both.

What this leaves out

Service from another CalPERS formula (miscellaneous years, for example) earns its own lower factor; myCalPERS handles that, this tool counts every year at your safety formula. A lateral to another agency, reciprocity with a county system, the Social Security offset some classic members have, purchased service, and survivor options are all outside it. So is anything your MOU does differently from the statute. The guide covers what each one means. The authority on your pension is CalPERS, and on your retiree health it's your MOU and HR.

This is a planning tool, not financial, legal, tax or retirement advice. Run the estimate in myCalPERS, call 888 CalPERS (888-225-7377), and get your separation and retirement dates confirmed in writing before you sign anything.
