The letter

Five markets, one set of rules.

Trendcraft follows the Nasdaq-100, gold, crude oil, the 7–10 year Treasury and bitcoin. Each signal comes from the plain market's own price, and the positions are held through 2x leveraged ETFs.

  • In or out. A market is held while it closes above its 200-day average and goes to cash when it closes below.
  • Built in steps. A new position starts at a quarter of full size and adds a quarter each time the market rises another full daily range above the entry.
  • Equal risk. Each market is sized to carry about the same risk, so the quiet Treasury gets the most dollars and bitcoin the fewest.
  • No opinions in the trades. The commentary is opinion. The positions never are.

The complete rules are on the Methodology page. If a rule ever changes, the change, the date and the reason are published there first.

Two editions, both free

A signal after every close. A recap every Saturday.

The daily arrives after every U.S. close: the model portfolio in a margin version and a cash version, every change and the reason for it, and where each market stands against its average.

The weekly arrives Saturday morning: the week's changes after the fact, where the book stands, and what moved markets and why.

Why rules

Judgment is where accounts die.

Most traders don't lose because their idea was wrong. They lose because they moved a stop after the trade went against them, doubled up to win it back, or skipped the next signal because the last one hurt. Each of those is a decision made under pressure, and pressure makes bad decisions.

A written rule removes that decision. The rule was set when I was calm, it was tested on years of data before anyone followed it, and on the day all that's left is to carry it out. That's harder than it sounds, because following a rule through a losing streak feels wrong every single time.

Taoism has a name for this: wu wei, acting without forcing. You stop fighting what the market is doing and let the rules respond to it.

What it is

A publication, not advice.

Every reader gets the same issue at the same time. It isn't personalized advice, and I never manage, trade or access a reader's account. I follow the cash version with my own money in a personal account, and I place those trades by hand only after the issue is sent. The full terms are on the Disclosures page.

This site doesn't post returns. The Track record page does, with every figure labeled for what it is and the backtest marked hypothetical.

On the side

Futures research.

I also build and test futures systems on the same standard: an explainable reason the rule should work, results that hold up on data it wasn't built on, and costs counted honestly. Most ideas fail that test. None of that work is published or offered here, and Trendcraft carries no futures signals.
