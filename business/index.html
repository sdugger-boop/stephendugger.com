Who this is for

Good businesses with a website that's letting them down.

Restaurants, trades, well and septic, auto and equipment repair, lodging, outfitters and shops. The work is good and the reviews prove it, but the website was built years ago and nobody has touched it since.

You probably need this if:

  • It's hard to read on a phone. Tiny text, pinch and zoom, buttons too small to tap.
  • Your menu or price list is a picture. Customers can't read it on a phone, and Google can't read it at all.
  • The footer still says 2013, or there's leftover template text nobody noticed.
  • The phone number is buried. People who want to call have to hunt for it.
  • You don't have a site at all, just a Yelp or Facebook page you didn't set up.
What you get

A site that sells the way you'd sell it in person.

  • Built from your real business. Your photos, your logo, your colors, your actual menu or services. No stock pictures of strangers.
  • Written to bring in calls. Plain words aimed at the people who actually buy from you, with your number one tap away on every screen.
  • Readable everywhere. Fast on a phone with one bar of signal, and every price and service in real text that Google can find.
  • Kept current. New hours, a price change, this week's special: send me a text and it's updated, usually the same day.
  • Yours. The domain and everything on the site stay in your name. If you ever leave, you take it all with you.
