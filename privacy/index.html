Privacy policy.

What this site collects, why, who else sees it, and how to have it removed.

This site is run by me, Stephen Dugger, in California. The short version: I don't run analytics or ad trackers, this site sets no cookies, and I don't sell or rent anyone's information. I collect only what it takes to sell you a plan, send you a newsletter you asked for, or answer your email.

What I collect, and why

When you buy a plan. Checkout runs on Stripe. Stripe collects your email address, your name and your card details. Your full card number goes to Stripe and never to me. From Stripe I receive your name, your email address, what you bought, the amount, and limited card details such as the last four digits and the card's country. I use that to deliver the plan, send your receipt, answer support questions, resend a lost download link, and keep the records tax law requires. I don't add buyers to any mailing list.

When you subscribe to the newsletter. The signup forms on this site send your email address to Ghost, the service that hosts and sends Trendcraft at trendcraft.ghost.io. Your address is used to send the newsletter, and every issue has an unsubscribe link. Ghost's own privacy terms cover how it stores the list.

When you email me. Your message and address stay in my mailbox so I can reply and keep track of the conversation.

When you use the free tools. The calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are never sent to me or anyone else. "Email me my copy" opens your own mail app with the result addressed to you; nothing passes through this site.

What other services see

Loading any web page shares some technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, with the services that serve it. For this site those are:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and may log visitor IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr, which serves the newsletter signup form on the pages that have one.
  • Stripe, when you open a checkout page.

Each one handles that information under its own privacy policy. I don't receive it.

Download pages

Each plan's download page has an unlisted address that isn't published or indexed by search engines. Anyone who has the link can open it, so keep yours to yourself.

How long I keep it

Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Email is kept as long as it's useful for answering you. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

Wherever you live, you can email me to ask what I hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. I'll do it, except for records the law requires me to keep. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time from the link in any issue.

Children

This site and its plans are for adults. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top. Questions go to steve@stephendugger.com. The terms of service cover purchases and refunds.
