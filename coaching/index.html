Training  ·  The Sovereign Method

Discipline without integration is just a faster way to run in the wrong direction.

A training method for people who already have discipline. What they don't have yet is a body that matches the rest of their life.

Who this is for

You're not lazy. You're fragmented.

You run a business, a career or a household well. You show up for other people. But your training and eating keep restarting every Monday, and each restart ends in the same place. Standard fitness advice assumes a simple life and a simple brain, and yours is neither. Maybe you're wired with ADHD, maybe you carry three jobs' worth of responsibilities, maybe your path has never been a straight line.

The problem usually isn't effort. Your self-image has a set point, like a thermostat. Push past it and something pulls you back to "normal." So the method changes two things at once: the training that changes your body, and the identity that decides what your body keeps returning to. Change only one and the other drags it back.

How the method works

Rules, not motivation.

  • The plan is decided before the week starts. What you lift, what you eat and what you do on a bad day are written down in advance, so a hard Tuesday doesn't get to renegotiate them.
  • Weekly averages, never single weigh-ins. Your weight swings two to five pounds a day from water and food. One number is noise. The trend is the signal.
  • A missed day is data, not failure. It tells you what in the plan doesn't fit your life yet. You adjust the plan, not your opinion of yourself.
  • Progress is checked against the record. The log, the averages and the lifts decide whether it's working, not how you feel on a given morning.
  • No macro gimmicks or medical claims. If something needs a doctor, see one.
