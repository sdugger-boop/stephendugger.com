How the plans work

Rules, not motivation.

Most training plans fail in the second or third week, and usually not because the program was wrong. The week got hard, a session got skipped, and the next one got renegotiated. Every plan here runs on the same five rules, written so that a bad day doesn't need a decision.

  • The plan is decided before the week starts. You open the table and do what it says. Nothing gets picked in the gym.
  • Weekly averages, never single weigh-ins. Your weight swings two to five pounds a day from water and food. One number is noise. The seven-day average is the signal.
  • A missed day is data, not failure. It tells you what in the plan doesn't fit your life yet. You adjust the plan, not your opinion of yourself.
  • Log every set. Load, reps, and how many reps you had left. The plan works off the record, not memory.
  • Progress is checked against the record. A session that felt bad and beat last week's numbers was a good session.

No macro gimmicks and no medical claims. If something needs a doctor, see one.
